Open source · MIT · Windows

Awaaz: a voice agent that runs on your laptop, costs nothing, and asks before it acts.

Hold a key and speak in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Awaaz types for you in any app, or does the task: researches in Chrome, sets reminders, reads your screen, drafts emails, runs scheduled jobs and answers out loud. Message it from your phone on Telegram.

Get it on GitHub

Why I built it

The best AI assistants live in someone else's cloud, need a subscription, and get access to everything. I wanted the same habits (talk, delegate, get a morning brief) on my own 16 GB laptop with no GPU, for free, with the risky actions under my control.

Product decisions

DecisionWhy
Speech recognition always localYour voice is the most personal data; it never leaves the PC
Instant commands skip the model"YouTube kholo" in 0.4 s; most daily commands don't need AI
Local model by default, any cloud key optional$0 by default; a free Groq or Gemini key makes agent tasks fast
Approvals enforced in codeCommands, emails and invites need a Yes every time, even if a web page tricks the model
Agent uses its own Chrome profileYour normal browser, passwords and cookies stay untouched
Telegram, not WhatsAppNo server, no open port, and no risk of a banned number

How it works

Hold key → Whisper on your PC → text
  ├─ dictation: typed where your cursor is
  └─ agent: instant command, or
       ├─ your cloud key (Groq, Gemini, OpenAI, Claude…)
       │    tools: Chrome, screen, files, memory, schedules,
       │           PowerShell*, Gmail*, Calendar*
       └─ no key: Ollama on localhost
* asks you first, every time
