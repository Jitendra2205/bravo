# Demo video script (60–90 seconds)

Record the screen plus your voice. One take is fine; cut pauses afterwards.

| Time | Show | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8 s | Turn Wi-Fi off in the taskbar, on camera | "This is my AI agent. It's running entirely on this laptop. Watch me turn the internet off." |
| 8–25 s | Hermes terminal, voice on | Speak: "What's on my brief today?" — it reads reminders and changed files aloud |
| 25–40 s | Same | Speak in Hinglish: "Kal subah nau baje client ko invoice bhejne ka reminder lagao" — it asks to confirm, you say yes, the line appears in reminders.md |
| 40–55 s | Notepad, Handy hotkey | Dictate a two-line email; text appears as you speak |
| 55–70 s | README results table | "I tested it against a cloud model on 30 of my own tasks. Here's where free and local holds up, and where it doesn't." |
| 70–85 s | Architecture diagram | "Open source parts, my setup, skills and evaluation. Costs nothing to run. Repo link below." |

Tips: increase the terminal font, hide personal files and notifications, and never show `.env` or keys.
